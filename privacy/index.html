Privacy Policy

Everyday Deals with Vera
Effective date: August 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Everyday Deals with Vera social publishing integration (the “Service”) handles information when an authorized account owner connects a supported social media account, including TikTok.

1. Information We May Receive

When you authorize a social platform, the platform may provide information permitted by the scopes you approve. Depending on the enabled features, this may include basic account identifiers or profile information, authorization tokens, publishing permissions, upload or publishing status, and information needed to carry out the actions you request.

We may also process content and settings you choose to submit, such as videos, images, captions, hashtags, links, privacy selections, and other publishing instructions.

2. How We Use Information

We use information only as reasonably necessary to authenticate the authorized account, display or confirm the connected account, upload or publish content you request, check publication status, troubleshoot errors, protect the Service, and comply with applicable platform requirements and law.

3. TikTok Data

If TikTok is connected, TikTok data is used only for the functionality authorized by the account owner and the permissions approved during TikTok authorization. We do not use TikTok authorization to access unrelated information or to publish to accounts that have not granted permission.

4. Service Providers

We may rely on service providers that support authentication, automation, hosting, storage, security, or API connectivity. Those providers process information only as needed to provide their services and are subject to their own contractual and privacy obligations. Social platforms such as TikTok process information under their own privacy policies.

5. Selling or Sharing Personal Information

We do not sell personal information. We do not disclose personal information for unrelated advertising purposes. Information may be shared with service providers when required to operate requested features, or when disclosure is required by law, security needs, or protection of rights.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the Service, complete requested publishing actions, maintain security and records, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes. Authorization credentials may be invalidated or deleted when access is revoked or no longer needed.

7. Security

Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are used to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security, and users should also protect their own social-platform credentials and connected accounts.

8. Your Choices and Rights

You may revoke a connected social account through the applicable platform or integration settings. Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information. Requests can be made through the official Everyday Deals with Vera social media account associated with the Service.

9. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through this integration.

10. International Processing

Service providers and social platforms may process information in locations different from your own. Where required, appropriate safeguards are used consistent with applicable law and platform requirements.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Service, applicable law, or third-party platform requirements change. The effective date shown above identifies the current version.

12. Contact

Questions or privacy requests may be directed through the official Everyday Deals with Vera social media account associated with the Service.
