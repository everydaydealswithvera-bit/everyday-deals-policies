TikTok Integration

Everyday Deals with Vera uses TikTok developer products to connect an authorized TikTok account and support approved content publishing workflows.

TikTok Login Kit
Used to securely authorize the TikTok account through TikTok OAuth.
user.info.basic
Used to identify the authorized TikTok account and display basic profile information such as the account name and avatar.
Content selection and review
The account owner selects the shopping-deal video, reviews the caption and available publishing settings, and chooses whether to continue.
video.publish
Used only after the authorized account owner explicitly initiates the TikTok publishing action.

No content is published automatically. The authorized user controls the final publishing action.
